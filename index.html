          About IP-SAKTI Sahayak

          Democratizing Intellectual Property & Regulatory Literacy for Ayurveda Innovators across India.

          The Challenge (SIH Problem Statement 26045)

          Ayurveda is supported by a rich body of traditional knowledge and biological resources. However, innovators, researchers, MSMEs, and practitioners frequently struggle to:

          • Identify public domain Traditional Knowledge (TKDL) and avoid invalid patent claims.
          • Navigate complex statutory provisions like Section 3(p) of the Indian Patents Act.
          • Comply with National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) regulations.
          • Understand foreign market requirements (US FDA, EU EMA) for Ayurvedic exports.

          Our AI-Powered Solution

          IP-SAKTI Sahayak brings plant intelligence, traditional knowledge records, intellectual property guidelines, and regulatory frameworks into a unified, source-cited assistant.

          • Source Transparency: Every answer links to verifiable repositories.
          • Multilingual Equity: Accessible in English and regional Indian languages.
          • Risk Scoring: Instant visual indicators for novelty and legal compliance.
